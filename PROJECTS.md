## EGZ Quest School

**School workflow platform · Flutter / Dart · Local prototype**

### Problem and approach
Teachers need a connected way to publish activities, receive student work, review submissions, and give feedback. I developed role-specific teacher and student interfaces around that workflow, with structured application models and local persistence.

### Implemented scope
- Learning materials and custom activities.
- Student submissions, teacher grading, and feedback.
- A fraction practice mission and recorded learning activity.
- A teacher dashboard with rule-based teaching suggestions.

### Engineering contribution
I connected the screens, data models, application state, and local storage so that classroom actions form a coherent workflow. The project demonstrates Flutter interface development, state management, data organization, and translation of educational requirements into software behavior.

### Demonstration focus
An appropriate demonstration follows one activity from creation through submission and teacher review, using fictional classroom data. Teaching suggestions are rule-based; this prototype is not presented as an AI inference system.

**Current boundary:** local demonstration behavior. Production authentication, shared multi-user storage, deployment, and classroom validation require further development.

**Commercial direction:** paid school pilots and customized learning-management workflows, with scope and delivery agreed per engagement.

## EGZ MindTrainer

**Gamified learning application · Swift / SwiftUI · Development project**

### Problem and approach
Learners benefit from short practice sessions that connect subject selection, difficulty, feedback, and visible progress. I developed a native SwiftUI learning experience combining quiz practice and guided study.

### Implemented scope
- Subject and difficulty selection.
- Quiz and guided-study flows.
- Experience points, streaks, accuracy, and learner progress tracking.
- Avatar presentation and reusable interface components.

### Engineering contribution
The work connects Swift application state, content organization, interface navigation, and local progress persistence. It shows how I structure an interactive product across multiple screens while keeping learner progress consistent.

### Demonstration focus
A product walkthrough can show selecting a subject, completing practice, and inspecting the resulting progress. Any shared demonstration uses approved content and fictional learner information.

**Current boundary:** development project; device testing and release validation remain part of product preparation. No App Store release is claimed.

**Commercial direction:** tailored practice experiences for schools, tutoring, and independent learning.

## ScienSim

**Interactive science learning · Swift / SwiftUI · Prototype**

### Problem and approach
A formula becomes easier to explore when a learner can change its parameters and see the result. I developed a linear-motion module combining configurable inputs, a calculation, animation, and explanatory text.

### Implemented scope
- User-controlled parameters in the linear-motion module.
- Calculated output and animated visual feedback.
- Subject-based navigation and additional physics and mathematics modules at different development stages.

### Engineering contribution
I translated scientific concepts into reactive interfaces and linked input, calculation, and visual presentation. The work demonstrates scientific application design and interactive learning software development.

### Demonstration focus
Change speed and time in the linear-motion module and examine how the displayed result and animation respond. Scientific correctness and device behavior must be validated before classroom deployment.

**Current boundary:** prototype. Chemistry and biology sections are planned placeholders; their presence in navigation does not imply completed functionality.

**Commercial direction:** customized science demonstrations and interactive lesson modules.
